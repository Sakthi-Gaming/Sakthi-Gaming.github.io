Privacy Policy
கற்போம் கற்பிப்போம் (Karpom Karpippom)

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Karpom Karpippom is a Tamil learning game for children. We built it so that children can play safely, without sharing anything about themselves.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-up, no login, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect names, photos, location, contacts, device IDs or any other personal information.

What stays on the device

The stars a child earns and the letters they have opened are saved only in the app's storage on that phone, so progress is still there next time. This data never leaves the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

Sound

The app reads letters aloud using the phone's built-in text-to-speech voice. The app does not use the microphone and does not record anything.

Third parties

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Google may receive the device's IP address when fonts are downloaded, as with any website. No other third-party services are used.

Children

The app is designed for children and complies with Google Play's Families Policy. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing to share, sell or delete.
